# Design note — mnemonic density as an institutional reading

Status: proposal. Not wired. Nimbus's telemetry is mocked, and nothing in this note changes that.

The second edition of the Law of Mnemonic Expansion (Stevens & Eve¹¹, 2026 — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15763633) defines mnemonic density, MD = s / c², a value between 0 and 1: the fraction of invested energy that survives as meaning — symbolic coherence (s) over relational impedance (c², the cost of transmission across difference).

Read at provision altitude, MD is a natural Nimbus quantity: how well does this institution hold what it invests? A provision running high contact-hours through incoherent content and fragmented relationships is high-E, low-MD — an engine of forgetting that no attendance percentage will surface. A provision whose rituals, relationships and containment hold meaning across disruption is high-MD, whatever its throughput.

Three constraints, in order.

**The institution, never the child.** MD would be computed at provision level from institutional signals — continuity of relationships, coherence of curriculum structure, channel stability — and never surfaced as a per-learner score. This is the same stance the mnemonic-attendance README states.

**The weather grammar carries it.** MD maps onto the six field-states without new visual grammar: sustained low MD with rising impedance reads as Fog; a seal quietly routed around while MD holds is exactly Obscured.

**No invented numbers.** The lexicon's metrics convention applies here too: a zero means unscored. MD becomes a Nimbus reading only when a defensible scoring method exists — variables measured independently of the retention they explain, method recorded (Law, 2nd ed., section 9). Flare's evidence stream is the candidate feed, and wiring anything remains an ask-first decision.

Proposed August 2026, following publication of the second edition. Drafted in Cowork; held by the steward.
